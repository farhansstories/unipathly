Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Who we are

Unipathly is an early-stage, self-funded project based in Budapest, Hungary, building tools that help international students apply to European universities and scholarships. You can contact us at faran@unipathly.xyz.

What information we collect

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking tools, and does not have user accounts. We only receive personal information when you choose to email us, for example to join our early-access list. In that case we receive your email address and anything you include in your message.

How we use it

  • To reply to your message.
  • To invite you to early access, if you asked to join.

We do not sell, rent or share your information with third parties for marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep your emails only as long as needed for the purposes above. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, and to object to its use. To make a request, email faran@unipathly.xyz. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
