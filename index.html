The problem

Applying to study abroad shouldn't feel like guesswork

Every country, university and scholarship has its own rules, portals, translations and deadlines. Information is scattered and often only in the local language. One missing document can mean a rejected application or a lost scholarship.

Scattered information

Requirements are spread across university pages, national portals and regional scholarship agencies.

Complex documents

Translations, legalisations, equivalence declarations and income documents vary by country.

Hard deadlines

Admission, pre-enrolment, visa and scholarship deadlines overlap and are easy to miss.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Unipathly available now?

Not yet. We're building the first version and will open early access to a small group of students first. Email us to join the list.

Who can use Unipathly?

Any student who wants to study at a European university, especially students from outside the EU who face extra steps like document legalisation and visas.

Does it replace the official university websites?

No. Unipathly organises the process and points you to official sources, but universities and official portals always have the final word on requirements.

What do you do with my email?

We only use it to reply to you and to invite you to early access. We never sell or share it. See our privacy policy.
