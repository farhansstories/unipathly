Applying to study abroad shouldn't feel like guesswork

Every country, university and scholarship has its own rules, portals, translations and deadlines. Information is scattered across dozens of websites, often only in the local language. One missing document can mean a rejected application or a lost scholarship.

🧭

Scattered information

Requirements are spread across university pages, national portals and regional scholarship agencies.

📄

Complex documents

Translations, legalisations, equivalence declarations and income documents vary by country.

⏰

Hard deadlines

Admission, pre-enrolment, visa and scholarship deadlines overlap and are easy to miss.

What Unipathly does

One place to plan, prepare and track your applications to European universities.

🗺️

Admission roadmaps

Step-by-step guides for each country and program: from pre-enrolment and entrance tests to enrolment and visa.

✅

Personal document checklists

A checklist built for your nationality and target program, showing which documents need translation or legalisation.

🎓

Scholarship guidance

Find regional and university scholarships you may qualify for, and understand exactly what each one requires.

📅

Deadline tracker

All your admission, scholarship and visa dates in one timeline, with reminders before each one.

How it works

From "where do I start?" to a submitted application.

Who it's for

Students from outside the EU who want to study in Europe and need a clear, reliable guide through the process.

  • High-school graduates applying for bachelor's programs
  • Graduates applying for master's programs
  • Students who need a scholarship or fee reduction to study abroad
  • Families and advisors helping a student apply

I'm a student based in Budapest, Hungary. Going through university admissions and scholarship applications in Europe as an international student, I saw how much time is lost to scattered information, unclear document rules and missed deadlines.

I started Unipathly to build the guide I wish I'd had: a single, clear path from choosing a program to enrolling, for every student who wants to study in Europe.
